Revoke Google Access — Zumbot

If you want to disconnect Zumbot from your Google Account, you can revoke access at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

How to revoke access

  1. Open Google Account permissions.
  2. Find Zumbot in the list of connected apps and services.
  3. Click the Zumbot entry to view details.
  4. Select Remove Access and confirm.
  5. Optionally, contact us at info@zumbot.ru if you want us to delete related service data.

What happens next

After access is revoked, Zumbot can no longer read or update your Google Calendar on your behalf.

Any future booking automation that depends on Google Calendar integration will stop until you authorize access again.
